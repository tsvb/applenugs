Yeti, the rest of the shop

The first sheet drew the window display: Home, now playing, the pill and the Mac dashboard. This one walks the other surfaces, the ones a user meets daily, and holds it to the same stock: night shelves, snow light, sale stickers, one foil pink for anything that moves through time.

Two ideas carry most of it. The empty pack is the backing card with nothing in the bubble yet, and it becomes sign-in and every empty state. The label sticker is the price sticker cut square, for the offers a poster makes (Live, Free). Everything else is the first sheet's rules applied without new material. The tabs, copy and states here are the app's real ones; where the first sheet drew something the app doesn't do, it's listed at the bottom for a ruling.

How the system stretches

The first sheet’s rules hold everywhere. These are the only additions, and where each one stops.

  • The pack goes where something is playing. Home hero, iPhone now playing (including the Duo split), the Mac Now Playing window, the Mac miniplayer. Never on a list, a search result, a download, a poster, the panel or the pill.
  • The empty pack goes where nothing is yet. Sign-in (with the nameplate), Couldn’t reach nugs.net, and the empty states: downloads, favorites, videos, search before you type. An empty bubble is never animated and never holds a glyph.
  • A fourth sticker: the label sticker. Same sale stock, night ink, 4° off true, cut square. Only for offers a poster or row makes: Live and Free. Facts (4K, Audio, PPV) are plain frost-outlined tags, never stock.
  • Foil is time. Scrub, ring, sweep, focus, and now video resume progress, download progress and the search caret. Volume, toggles and selection aren’t time and stay snow or slush. Still never a fill: today’s pink-filled LIVE/FREE badges, poster play discs, the panel’s Queue button and the downloads play glyphs all change.
  • One primary per screen, snow with night ink: Sign in, Try again, Play all, Pause, Buy on nugs.net. Everything else is a frost ring or bare type.
  • Errors are said, not colored. Snow sentence, slush reason, raw error last in quiet monospace. Red is kept for destructive buttons, which are system-drawn anyway.
  • No eyebrows, no caps. Section names are the barker’s, heavy rounded, sentence case. Every uppercased eyebrow and the Shoebox-era caps go.
  • Casing. Buttons, toasts, headers and empty states are sentence case (Sign out, Try again). Menu items and system dialog buttons follow the platform’s Title Case (Sign Out, Reset Stats). Same words, two casings, one rule.
  • Not drawn here, and how they go. The Library’s Favorites segment: artist chips are shelf with a frost keyline (as on Mac Home), ShowCard is a flat square cover with a frost keyline, title heavy 15 and artist slush (the poster rule, cut square), and saved videos reuse the poster. The Mac’s Artists, Favorites, Downloads and Search are the iPhone rows in the detail column. The Mac sign-in and connection screens are the iPhone column centered in the window; the Mac offline sheet is Downloads with the transport bar docked. The Click Wheel and Receiver players aren’t restyled; they’re deleted at stage (e).
  • System surfaces we leave alone: AVKit’s player controls, the tab bar and accessory glass, menus, context menus, confirmation dialogs, alerts, Sparkle’s update window, share sheets and the window’s traffic lights. Forced dark mode makes them sit right; we own their words.

Reconciling the first sheet with the app

Walking the real screens turned up places where yeti-theme.html drew something the app doesn’t do. Each needs a ruling before the spec: recommend is my pick, question is yours to decide, keep means the first sheet yields to the app.

iPhone tabs keepThe sheet shows Home, Artists, Favorites, Downloads, Search. The app has Home, Artists, Search, Library (Favorites + Downloads), Videos. Following the sheet would drop Videos. This page uses the app’s tabs.
iPhone Home hero questionThe sheet puts the pack on iPhone Home, but the resume card is Mac-only today: iPhone Home is greeting, favorites, ledger, crate. Its label in the sheet reads “Newest show from artists you follow”, which is a feature the app doesn’t have. Is it (a) the resume card, brought to iPhone, or (b) a new “newest from who you follow” card?
Artist counts questionThe sheet’s Artists list shows “418 shows” per row. The catalog list gives a name and an id only, so counts would mean one request per artist. Drop the counts, or find a cheap source first?
Mac sidebar keepThe sheet shows Home, Artists, Favorites, Search plus a “Following” list of artists. The app has Home, Artists, Videos, Favorites, Downloads, Search. Keep the app’s items; a Following group would be a new feature and can wait.
Now-playing counter questionThe sheet says “Set 2, track 3 of 6”; the app says “Track 3 of 24” and the queue doesn’t know about sets. Set-aware means carrying set data into the queue. Worth it, or keep “Track 3 of 24”?
Signal rows keepThe sheet shows five rows, with “Source: Soundboard”. The app’s six constant rows (Format, Platform tier or Source, Sample rate, Bit depth, Channels, Buffered) are a layout invariant. Restyle only.
Frost-gray traffic lights recommendReal window buttons can’t be recolored without replacing them. Drop it; dark mode is enough.
Mac transport bar recommendMissing from the sheet’s Mac; drawn here. It also takes the play sticker, which makes four play-sticker sites, not three.
