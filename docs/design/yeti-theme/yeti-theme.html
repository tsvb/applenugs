Yeti

The sixth theme, and the first one about a song's world rather than a piece of playback gear. Goose's "Yeti" is sung by a deadpan street barker selling the arrival of a creature that will sort everybody into naughty and nice, out on a cold street full of cheap gadgets. The theme takes the shop window from that scene: your cover art sealed in a clear plastic bubble on a backing card, hung on a peg like a vintage action figure.

Everything else stays night-quiet, because a show runs three hours. Snow is the light, never a picture. Yellow sale stickers mark the lossless badge, the play button and the playing row. One pink, borrowed from foil-printed posters, is kept for the progress ring, the scrub bar and a single sweep across the title when a new track comes out of its box. Home's listening ledger becomes the nice list.

Stickers

Every sticker is the same stock: sale yellow, night ink, a few degrees off true. Pink is never printed on one.

FoilSweep, the one moment

A band of foil pink crosses the now-playing title once, left to right, when a new track starts. It lasts 1,020 ms on a standard ease-in-out, then the title rests in snow for the rest of the track.

  • Trigger. The first time playback is running for a new player.current. A track change fires it; pause then resume does not, so a three-hour show with ten pauses sees it once per track. A launch-restored queue fires on its first play, not at launch.
  • Reads no playback time. It keys on the track's identity and runs its own animation. It never reads currentTime, so it adds nothing to the 4 Hz tick.
  • Overlay only. The band is drawn over the title's own glyphs (a masked gradient), so the title never changes size or wraps differently mid-pass.
  • Reduce Motion. Skipped entirely: the title simply appears in snow.
  • Where. The now-playing title on the iPhone card and the Mac miniplayer. Nowhere else.

Wiring it in

  • ThemeID. Add case yeti, display name "Yeti", and the Theme.make branch.
  • labelPaper and labelInk are documented as Tape Room only; only the cassette miniplayer reads them, so Yeti can reuse them for card stock and sticker ink. Update the comments.
  • Playing row tint is free. activeEmphasis returns playState for a static accent, so setlist and queue rows go sale yellow with no code change.
  • Row sticker. EqualizerBars animates three bars in the accent for every .equalizerRows theme (queue, now playing, crate header). Yeti needs a branch there that draws the static sticker stair, or it gets bouncing pink bars.
  • Play sticker. The Mac mini's .filled triad paints its circle in the accent, which would be a pink fill; it needs a sticker style (playState fill, labelInk glyph). Now playing and the Home hero draw *.circle.fill glyphs that need the same. If that is too much, use the fallback on the sticker sheet.
  • New components. BlisterBubble (hero, now playing, Mac mini art only) and FoilSweep.
  • Focus. The foil focus outline is drawn per control; the system focus ring follows the user's accent color, so this needs a custom focus effect on macOS.
